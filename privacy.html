Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Colum Dedup is a Chrome extension that hides videos on YouTube. This policy explains what it stores, where that data lives, and who it is shared with.

The short version

  • What the extension learns about your viewing stays in your browser. It is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or ads, and we don't sell data.
  • If you subscribe to Pro, payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe.

Data stored in your browser

To do its job, the extension saves the following using Chrome's built-in extension storage on your computer:

WhatWhy
IDs of videos you've clicked from YouTube feeds and search resultsTo hide videos you've already opened
How many times each video has appeared in your feedTo hide repeats that keep showing up
Videos you've hidden, with their title and channel nameTo keep them hidden and list them so you can undo it
Your search terms, and which channels you clicked or skipped for eachSmart search ranking (Pro); it can be turned off in Settings
A daily count of how many videos were hidden (last 14 days)The "Hidden this week" number
Your settings, blocked words, blocked channels and chosen colorsTo remember your preferences
Whether you have Pro, and when your free Pro week endsTo unlock Pro features

Older entries are trimmed automatically so this data doesn't grow forever. "Clear All" in the extension deletes your history and hidden videos, and uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored locally.

The date your free Pro week started is saved in Chrome's sync storage. If you use Chrome sync, Chrome copies that date to your other signed-in browsers through your Google account, so the free week isn't restarted on each one.

Services the extension contacts

  • YouTube. The extension runs on youtube.com pages you visit and changes what's shown there. The extension's menu loads video thumbnails from YouTube's image server (i.ytimg.com), the same images YouTube itself shows you.
  • ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com). Used only for Pro subscriptions. It checks whether you've paid, and if you subscribe or log in, it receives your email address. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card details. ExtensionPay and Stripe handle that data under their own privacy policies.

Your viewing history, hidden videos, search terms and settings are not sent to either service.

Permissions

  • Storage: to save the data listed above in your browser.
  • Tabs: so the extension's menu can tell your open YouTube tabs to refresh after you change something.
  • Access to youtube.com: to hide videos and page sections on YouTube.
  • Access to extensionpay.com: so the extension knows when a Pro payment has gone through.

Children

Colum Dedup isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.
