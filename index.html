faq

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The free features keep working forever, with no account. Pro is an optional subscription, and every new install gets Pro free for the first 7 days.

What does it do with my data?

What it learns about your viewing stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere. The only outside service it uses is ExtensionPay, which handles Pro subscriptions. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it work on my phone?

No. It's a Chrome extension, so it works in Chrome on Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS.

I hid something by mistake. How do I get it back?

Open the extension from your toolbar. Recently hidden videos are listed with an Unhide button, and Settings has the full list.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the extension, go to Settings, and click "Manage subscription". You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.
